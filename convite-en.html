How to invite someone

The easiest way to start a conversation on Seixo: you send a link, the other person taps it and the conversation opens. Nobody needs to copy IDs.

To invite someone

  1. On the conversation list, tap "Invite someone".
  2. Choose how to send it: a text message, email, another chat app, whatever suits you.
  3. That's it. When the other person accepts, the conversation appears in your list.

If you receive an invite

  1. If you already have Seixo: tap the link. Seixo opens and asks "Start a conversation?". Tap "Start".
  2. If you don't have it yet: tap the link and a page opens with an "Open in the App Store" button. Install Seixo and create your account (just open the app: no phone number or email needed).
  3. Once your account is ready, tap the link again (or, on the page that opened, "Open the invite in Seixo"). The conversation starts.

Good to know

  • Each invite works once and expires after 7 days. For someone else, make another invite.
  • Under the button, "See your invites" shows whether each one has been used, and lets you share it again or cancel it.
  • Whoever has the link can use it, so send it only to the person you want to invite. If you sent it by mistake, cancel it.
  • We don't know who you send an invite to or who used it: the server only knows that it was used.
