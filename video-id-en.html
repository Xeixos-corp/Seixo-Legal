How to talk to someone on Seixo

Seixo has no usernames and no phone numbers. To talk to someone, one of you just needs the other's ID.

The steps

  1. One person opens “My ID”, on the conversation list.
  2. If you are together: the other person taps “Scan QR” and points the camera at the code.
  3. If you are far apart: the first person taps “Share my ID” and sends it any way they like. The other copies the message, then taps “Paste” and “New”.
  4. The conversation opens. After that, either of you can write first.
